About

What we built.

Diffusion models have emerged as a popular approach for image and video synthesis because of their ability to model complex, high-dimensional data through an iterative denoising process.

We recreated Stable Diffusion from the ground up in PyTorch: the VAE, the U-Net, the CLIP text encoder, the forward process, and five samplers.

The samples on this page were rendered end to end through our own code, using converted Stable Diffusion 1.4 weights. The implementation page walks through each module.
